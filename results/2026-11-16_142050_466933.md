A Bronze Age civilization on the island of Crete 

The Minoans were the people of a remarkable Bronze Age culture centered on Crete, the large island south of mainland Greece. # **Sailors and Mediterranean Trade** 

How Crete connected the Bronze Age world 

Crete sits near the crossroads of the Aegean and eastern Mediterranean. Minoan sailors used this geography to move pottery, raw materials, and valuable products between islands and distant coastal communities. 

Contacts reached the Cyclades, mainland Greece, Egypt, Anatolia, and the Levant. Exports probably included textiles, agricultural products such as olive oil, timber, and finely made objects. Imports included copper, tin, ivory, precious metals, and luxury materials.
