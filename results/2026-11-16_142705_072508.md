## Question

What was the Danelaw?

## Answer

The Danelaw was a term used to describe the political and legal geography of areas in England under Danish influence following Viking settlements, particularly after the 9th-century agreements between King Alfred of Wessex and Viking leader Guthrum. It encompassed regions such as Northumbria, East Anglia, and Mercia, where Danish customs and legal traditions became prominent. However, it was not a single, permanent Viking state but a region with evolving boundaries and institutions.
