The longship and knarr differed primarily in design and purpose. Longships were long, narrow, and shallow-drafted, allowing them to navigate rivers, beaches, and rough seas, and were used for warfare, exploration, and raids. In contrast, knarrs were broader and specifically designed as cargo vessels for transporting goods, animals, and supplies across open water.
